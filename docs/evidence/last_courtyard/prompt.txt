Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: architectural concept target for a modular hex-tile first-person game environment.
Create a beautiful, precise 3D architectural diorama called The Last Courtyard, in The Thin district: an abandoned minimalist timber pavilion complex built by people who decided to own almost nothing. No text in image.
Composition: elevated three-quarter view, entire compact composition visible against a dark muted background. Six hexagonal floor modules form a ring around ONE deliberately empty central hexagonal court over dark void. One short entrance pavilion attaches at the front. The ring makes two continuous walkable routes around the court. An exposed straight timber-supported ramp on the right rises one full storey to a single upper lookout pavilion at the rear-right. Clearly show where the ramp starts and ends. Regular hex module boundaries expressed subtly in deck edges.
Architecture: slender paired dark timber posts, low broad pitched roofs with deep eaves contained over their modules, countable rafters beneath them, sparse pale paper-like screen panels at the outer margins, mostly open sides. Flat clean timber decks, flush connecting thresholds, low restrained edging at the court; upper lookout has an open front. Alternate roofed pavilions and open decks so the central empty space and paths are legible from above. Delicate joinery and quiet restraint; one asymmetrically placed screen marks the entrance. No tall concrete pillars, no repeating wells inside modules, no furniture clutter.
Gameplay architecture: unobstructed connected routes, long sightlines across the court, little concealment, a real exposed climb with space to walk, a clear empty upper destination for a possible rescue or summit approach. Architecture only, no actors or UI or glowing markers.
Materials and mood: weathered dark wood, pale desaturated screens, dim warm practical lamps tucked beneath beams, cool dusk shadows. Crafted game-ready convex geometry with crisp bevels and planar roof slopes, no curved ornament, no landscape or vegetation. Architectural clarity and one coherent empty courtyard matter more than surface detail. Landscape image, cinematic but legible lighting.
