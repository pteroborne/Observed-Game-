Create a refined, cohesive architectural diorama of nineteen connected hexagonal cells arranged as one compact, roughly hexagonal field, all on ONE storey. It must read as one continuous back-of-house interior, rather than a chain of separate pavilions. Every cell is about fourteen metres across, floor slab half a metre thick, and a low suspended ceiling about three metres above the floor. The hidden hexagonal boundaries organize the model but should not dominate the room interiors.
Show a selective architectural roof removal over the foreground and centre, preserving a broad coherent section of the suspended ceiling across the rear third. This reveals the underside of the remaining ceiling: repetitive rectangular cream acoustic panels, shallow straight grid rails, and sparse fluorescent housings in a rhythm of one illuminated panel per three. Squared, plain columns repeat on a sober orthogonal rhythm. Broad openings between rooms have flush plain edges without decorative architraves or doors. Muted yellow-beige plaster, slightly darker worn neutral floor, grey ceiling joints. No signs, no props, no furniture, no people.
The circulation forms several short loops. One off-centre solid partition interrupts a direct view while leaving broad passage around its ends; identical passages reappear beyond it. One nearby cell has a real modest rectangular maintenance opening in its floor, with a narrow straight crossing through it and a wider route around the perimeter. Show the black empty space in that opening clearly. Keep the rest of the floor continuous, level and physically connected. These features support route choice, observation and retreat, with no overt gameplay icons or machinery.
High three-quarter axonometric view of the whole compact composition, generous charcoal negative space around all sides, no cropping. Restrained cinematic warm fluorescent light, enough ambient illumination to read the floor and openings. Atmosphere of anonymous repeated service space and disorientation, not horror gore or supernatural creatures. Precise brush-buildable planar geometry, modest chamfers, restrained material texture; prioritize coherent massing, clear circulation and achievable construction over photoreal weathering. No outer railings, neon strips, arrows, map lines, visible grid overlay, captions or HUD.
