Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original game-environment concept for a WFC hex-tile modelling benchmark, landscape.
Primary request: "The Uncalled Number", an empty Institutional district admissions hall in Architect Ascent. A polished architectural cutaway model composed of fifteen connected hexagonal room modules, entirely on one continuous floor.
Scene: two parallel long corridors linked by three short reception passages, forming two loops. One is the public side: built-in rows of bench seating, a broad arrival, orderly low queue islands. The other is the staff side: recessed built-in storage cabinets and offset opaque partitions. The three connecting passages contain simple service counters beneath rectangular OPEN hatches in opaque partitions: a person can see through a hatch at eye height, but must walk around the end of the partition to pass through. Clear circulation around everything. No active doors or game characters.
Architecture and materials: a deserted civic clinic or government admissions office, pale grey-green washable walls, restrained darker green dado panels, a continuous grey terrazzo-like floor, thick utilitarian door reveals, small bevels, recessed fluorescent strip fixtures in flat low ceilings. Wall-backed benches with solid bases. Low queue dividers, NOT rail mazes. No wood, paper screens, brutalist monument, ornamental sculpture, loose furniture or clutter.
Composition: elevated three-quarter architectural cutaway showing the complete elongated double-corridor footprint, long axis mostly horizontal, with generous dark background margin. Remove near roofs and some foreground wall sections to expose counters, circulation and benches; keep a few rear low ceiling panels with embedded light strips. Every mass physically supported. Make the service hatches and contrast between public benches and staff cabinets unmistakable.
Lighting: cool-white fluorescent pools with a faint green institutional cast; carefully controlled brightness, visible contact shadows and legible paths. Quiet, orderly, unsettling absence. Museum-quality architectural model photography, clean silhouettes and excellent spatial clarity.
Constraints: achievable using a small kit of convex primitives. No people, text, numbers, signage, logos, symbols, water, plants, stairs, glass reflections, neon accents or visual grid overlay. No floating objects. This is a scene concept, not a diagram or UI.
