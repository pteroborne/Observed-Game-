Use case: concept-art. Create one polished architectural game environment concept for "The Borrowed View", in the Shadow Screen district of Observed 2. This image will be translated into real reusable WFC hex tiles, so make the geometry coherent, buildable and countable.

A compact composition of eleven contiguous pointy-top hexagonal rooms, each about 14 by 16 metres with a standard 8 metre storey, arranged as a long central gallery with two short looping side routes. Single level, no stairs. Cutaway architectural model at a generous oblique three-quarter angle on a charcoal background, fill the frame, show the actual interior. Leave a few rear roof panels intact, remove the foreground roofs and selectively section the near walls to make the routes legible.

Shadow Screen is high contrast rather than simply dark: near-black stained timber floors and horizontal members, luminous warm ivory paper wall panels. Restrained, contemporary Japanese architectural influence, no pagodas, no ornament, no furniture. Within the main gallery are paired offset freestanding horizontal slat screens. Each screen is a simple stack of five or six thick horizontal timber rails, with real narrow see-through gaps between them, not an opaque rectangle pretending to be a screen. Screens meet short pale paper partitions at their ends; staggered openings lead around those partitions, and one route is visible through the slats but not directly accessible. Keep wide walkable thresholds between hex cells and clearly connected side loops. A quieter terminal alcove provides a glimpse back along the overlapping screens. Distinguish bright wall planes, near-black horizontal slats, and the dark matte floor. Modest large overhead crossbeams, sparse concealed warm rectangular practical light housings. Fine paper lattice may be surface texture; the main slats must have visible thickness and cast shadows.

Composition prioritizes repeated screens receding into depth, a readable central route, side paths and framed partial views. Beautiful raking warm light through the horizontal gaps, softened shadows, clear forms and sufficient interior exposure to understand the model. Physically plausible architectural visualization, precise edges with restrained bevels, material richness, polished environment art benchmark. No people, characters, machinery, weapons, lantern props, signs, text, labels, logos, water, foliage or standalone scattered tiles. Architecture and light only.
