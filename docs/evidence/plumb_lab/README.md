# Plumb — which way is down

Evidence for [`labs/plumb_lab`](../../../labs/plumb_lab/README.md): a
feasibility check for a tool that redirects one body's gravity.

![Hanging from the ceiling](ceiling.png)

A redirected gravity puts the subject on every surface of the room, holds it
there, and it walks along each one. The red arrow is the direction the plumb
calls down; the green line is the surface normal it is actually resting on.

The recording is a cutaway: the two walls between the camera and the room are
omitted from presentation only. They are still in the collision world, and the
subject stands on one of them.
