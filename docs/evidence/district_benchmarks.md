# District modelling benchmarks

Ten concept-first studies, each translated into reusable WFC sources and a
hand-composed Hex Tile Lab scene. The final study is Institutional, completed
15 September 2026. Names below distinguish the game's technical architecture
registers; they do not introduce new fictional district canon.

The series expands the authored catalogue from 354 to 428 sources. Placements
count scene instances, including reused existing towers; they are not counts of
rotated WFC candidates or occupied logical cells. The Empty Audience authors
nine designs and uses eight of them in its featured composition.

Each evidence page preserves its generated concept, lab views, source details,
validation and known gaps. These scenes demonstrate reusable geometry and
spatial affordances. They do not guarantee the WFC solver will assemble the
featured landmark or implement new gameplay behaviour. The final Institutional
verification ledger records the full workspace check after all ten studies.
