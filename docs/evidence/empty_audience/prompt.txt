Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: concept target for a buildable modular hex-tile game environment.
Create The Empty Audience, in The Welcome / Facet Monument district. It is one coherent ceremonial building built for an important guest who never arrived. Empty architecture; no people, throne, statues, writing, UI, or symbols.
View: landscape architectural diorama, elevated three-quarter view from the entrance, the entire compact building visible against a dark charcoal background. Clearly legible geometry, sophisticated restrained game environment art.
Layout: a long straight central processional axis of about six hexagonal floor modules. At its midpoint it splits to two symmetrically flanking walkable ramps, each climbing exactly one storey. The two ramps flank a lower central axis and join upper landings, converging on ONE raised empty dais at the back. The dais sits on a substantial connected lower structure. A short narrow bridge on the lower axis spans a genuine small dark gap. Roughly thirteen connected hex modules altogether, two storeys. Subtle hexagonal seams on the foundation, but visually one connected monument, never a scattering of independent islands.
Architectural focal point: the upper rear dais is framed by three deeply stepped, angular portal reveals, growing wider and taller toward the observer, with integrated light concealed in the joints. A clean empty faceted podium is reached by a short gentle approach ramp. Surrounding architecture remains lower and quieter. The processional walls step outward in three horizontal terraces as they rise. Angled wedge buttresses, chamfered limestone piers, recessed reveals and long disciplined bands guide the eye toward the dais. Roofless central processional space so the paths remain visible. No circular wells or pavilion roofs.
Materials: warm desaturated limestone, bronze-brown structural shadows, modest warm-white joint lights. Broad planar surfaces, precise bevels, crisp obtuse angles, heavy yet elegant massing. Limited ornament; all interest comes from mass, subtraction, proportion and light in joints.
Gameplay clarity: show uninterrupted walkable entry, two physically continuous ascents with usable landings, observation between flanks and across the central route, and the real exposed bridge. Unobstructed lanes; no decorative object blocking a threshold. No fantastical disconnected stairs. The scene should be ambitious but reconstructible with convex solid geometry and modular hex tiles.
